Winter at the park: Groomed, tracked, and ready to ski.

Nestled in the boreal forest of the Humber Valley, Pasadena Ski and Nature Park offers a unique environment for cross country skiing, snowshoeing and nature interpretation.

Six trails, all from the chalet

Start short on Jackrabbit or go long on Caribou. Every loop brings you back to the wood stove.

  • JackrabbitBeginner, good for a night ski1 km
  • Lone PineNovice, past the owl boxes2.5 km
  • Looper RunIntermediate, Deer Lake view2.5 km
  • MartenIntermediate, rolling hills3 km
  • TwistedChallenging, the fire pit3 km
  • CaribouIntermediate, Long Range view5 km

New, corrected trail maps (and GPS files) are on the way and will go up on the Our Trails page.

The chalet

Warm up at the wood stove, rent skis or snowshoes, and grab something at the canteen. It hosts weddings, reunions and team meetings too.

  • OpenStaffed 9–5, seven days a week in winter
  • HeatWood stove and electric heat
  • SpaceVeranda on three sides, upper deck, an acre of field for tents
  • FoodKitchen for catering, canteen and bar service
