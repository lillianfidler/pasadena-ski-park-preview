Nestled in the boreal forest of the Humber Valley, Pasadena Ski and Nature Park offers a unique environment for cross country skiing, snowshoeing and nature interpretation.

Pick your loop

Every trail starts and ends at the chalet. Jackrabbit is right beside it; Caribou takes you out to the view of the Long Range Mountains.

  • Jackrabbit1 kmBeginner. Gentle and rolling, close to the chalet, good for a night ski.
  • Lone Pine2.5 kmNovice. Counter-clockwise loop past the owl boxes.
  • Looper Run2.5 kmIntermediate. The club's first trail, with a view of Deer Lake.
  • Marten3 kmIntermediate. Rolling hills and a glide back to the fire.
  • Twisted3 kmChallenging. Hills, yellow birch, and the fire pit.
  • Caribou5 kmIntermediate. Wide open sky over the North Harbour Valley.

New, corrected trail maps (and GPS files) are on the way and will land on the Our Trails page.

Back to the stove

Warm up by the wood stove, rent your skis or snowshoes, and grab a bite at the canteen. The chalet hosts weddings, reunions and team meetings too.

  • Staffed 9–5, seven days a week in winter
  • Wood stove and electric heat
  • Veranda on three sides, upper deck
  • Kitchen and canteen, accessible

The forest, all year

When the snow goes, the brooks and bogs come alive. Henry Mann, a club member and retired biology professor, wrote fifteen field guides to what you'll find here, from mushrooms to winter birds. Seventy-one bird species have been recorded in the park.

  • Spring Wildflowers
  • Winter Birds
  • Mushrooms
  • Lichens
  • Moss Gardens of Kay's Hill
