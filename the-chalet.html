The Chalet

Book The Chalet for your next event

Are you looking for a venue for a special occasion? The chalet is available to host special events – weddings, reunions, team meetings. Contact us to discuss.

Amenities

  • Accessible
  • Extensive field area ~1 acre, mowed for events
  • Suitable for party tents
  • Kitchen, available for catering
  • Canteen / Bar service
  • Wood stove and electric heat
  • Large veranda that wraps around 3 sides, upper level deck
